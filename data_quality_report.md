## Principles applied

1. Evidential measurements (speed, confidence scores, image quality, latency) are never imputed. Invalid readings are nulled and flagged, because an imputed speed cannot support a citation.
2. Descriptive categories receive an explicit Unknown level rather than a guessed value.
3. Every repaired or contradictory field keeps its original value in a companion column.
4. Ambiguous slash dates are resolved against the date window established by unambiguous records and otherwise default to month first, with a per row ambiguity flag.
